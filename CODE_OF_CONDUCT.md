# Code of Conduct

VSIX Scout contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Use considerate, inclusive language.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and security disclosure boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Reports can be sent privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/5Reeson/vsix-scout/security/advisories/new).
Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and consistently as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
